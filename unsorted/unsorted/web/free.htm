mostly our most recently-produced publications.

offered for-sale in paper.

Items in Blue are posted online only (for free) and are
not offered as part of paper-version sales.

Items in Red are posted online here AND are currently
(Nov. 2015) ALSO part of paper-version sales.

Items in Green are miscellaneous, often not currently printed,
or were never issued in paper (see descriptions).

So if you are printing your own hard copies from this
page, AND ordering some material from us as well,
wait to see if they are part of any hard copy sets you order.

Nearly all newsletters and app notes not posted are available
as paper copies - see Prices/Shipping.
