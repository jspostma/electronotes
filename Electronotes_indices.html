Electronotes Newsletter

Electronotes Newsletter contains information related to the technical details of music synthesis and other sound processing, both equipment and methods and news, for readers who are at the level of a junior electrical engineering student or have a similar background.

Volume 1 to 4 were titled Electronotes Newsletter, volume 5 onwards were titled Electronotes: Newsletter of the Musical Engineering Group[1]

Note: The 556 is the Motorola MC1456/MC1556 op-amp, not the dual 555 timer. Any op-amp with at least 2.5V/μs slew rate is suitable. The LF351 should work in its place.[2]

Some of these are reprinted in the Builder's Guide and Preferred Circuits Collection in 1986.

Builder's Guide and Preferred Circuits Collection

Builder's Guide and Preferred Circuits Collection is a collection of the best EN analog circuits up to July 1986. These are circuits suggested for construction.

"Because of the way the BG&PCC has evolved over the years, the style, format, and amount of accompanying text varies from item to item. A few diagrams are included to more or less complete a "Catalog" of circuits, and the accompanying text may begin and/or end mid-sentence. Most items now include the full, original text, but don't be alarmed by the few that don't."
